The performer supplies the answer. RAJU TELLER does not read minds, use AI to discover secrets, or predict the future. It is for entertainment, not medical, legal, financial or other consequential decisions.

The free version may show banner ads after applicable privacy choices. A one-time store purchase removes ads. Price is shown by your store before you approve payment. Restore purchases is available in Settings. No subscription is offered by this design.
